Every SCRUB product is designed for a specific water body type and deployment scenario — from still urban lakes to fast-flowing rivers. They operate independently or as a coordinated fleet.

V1

V2
Passive Intake
Robot

A conveyor-less design that uses hydrodynamics to collect waste — no actuators, no motors for intake. Forward motion opens the door; reversing closes it. Simpler, lower-cost, lower-maintenance.

—Zero intake power consumption
—Minimal moving parts · low maintenance
—Lightweight · easily transportable
—Scalable foundation for autonomous fleets
Developed & Validated
V3
River Waste
Interceptor

A static shore installation for rivers and canals. Anchored at pollution chokepoints, it uses a floating boom to funnel waste into an inclined conveyor — delivering debris directly to the riverbank. No vessel required.

—Static installation · no boat required
—100% solar powered · continuous operation
—Floating boom guides waste to intake
—Modular · scalable across pollution hotspots
In Development

The Problem

India's urban water bodies
are in crisis.

India has 2.4 million water bodies — yet 16.3% are non-functional due to pollution and encroachment, and 45.2% have never been repaired or maintained. The 69,485 urban water bodies face the sharpest pressure.

Current remediation relies on manual labour: slow, hazardous, seasonal, and producing no usable data on water health. Municipal bodies have no continuous, scalable, or autonomous alternative.

The National Green Tribunal has issued hundreds of orders against civic bodies for lake pollution. BBMP faces court-ordered deadlines for water quality compliance — demand for an autonomous solution is no longer optional.

India's smart water management market is growing at 16.4% CAGR through 2030. There is currently no domestically built, commercially deployed autonomous lake-cleaning robot in the country.

01

Autonomous Navigation

Grid-based GPS pathfinding with AI obstacle avoidance. SCRUB covers a defined lake zone without human intervention, returning to dock when full.

02

Real-Time Water Quality

pH, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, TDS, and temperature — continuously sensed, GPS-tagged, and streamed live to a cloud dashboard accessible to BBMP officers.

03

Solar-Assisted Operation

Zero fuel cost. Solar charging extends operation through a full working day with zero emissions and zero chemical footprint.

04

AI Debris Classification

Onboard AI vision continuously scans the surface, distinguishing plastic, foam, and organic debris in real time — validated at 90%+ precision on real lake footage.

05

Aquatic-Safe Collection

A precision mesh conveyor lifts waste aboard while its aperture is sized to let aquatic life pass through unharmed. Field-proven at Kengeri Lake.

06

Dual-Mode Control

Fully autonomous AI navigation or instant manual RC override at any time — critical for safety near swimmers.

How We Work

Flexible engagement
models for every partner.

Whether you're a municipal body, a corporate with CSR mandates, or a research institution — SCRUB has a model that fits.

RaaS
Robots as a Service
Recurring · Monthly or Annual

SCRUB owns, deploys, and maintains the hardware. You pay a monthly lease — zero capital expenditure, full operational coverage.

CaaS
Cleaning as a Service
Recurring · Per Lake Contract

Annual contract for scheduled lake cleaning. SCRUB provides crew and bots on a defined frequency — weekly or bi-weekly. Ideal for municipalities and lake trusts.

WaaS
Water Intelligence as a Service
Recurring · Per Site

SaaS subscription to SCRUB's environmental dashboard — pH, TDS, turbidity, DO, and more, GPS-tagged and streamed in real time. For researchers, industries, and compliance reporting.

Direct Sale
Bot Purchase
One-Time · Hardware

Outright purchase of a SCRUB unit. Suited to corporates, private resorts, ports, and research institutions who prefer to own and operate their own system.

AMC
Annual Maintenance Contract
Recurring · Per Unit

12-month comprehensive service covering predictive maintenance, OTA firmware updates, sensor recalibration, and spare parts. For bot owners.

Data Licensing
Research & Compliance Data
Per Dataset · Annual

Aggregate, anonymised lake health data licensed to municipal planners, water research institutes, and environmental consultancies for planning and compliance.
